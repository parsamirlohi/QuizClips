What we collect

Waitlist: your email address and the plan you picked. Settings: your choices (video type, voice, look and so on) are saved in your own browser, not on our servers. Videos: the videos you generate are stored on our server so you can download them.

Who else sees your input

To make a video, the topic, script text and search terms are sent to services we use: Google (Gemini) to write scripts and check footage, Pixabay to find footage, and a text-to-speech provider to record the narration. Don't enter personal or confidential information as a topic.

Safety checks

Topics, text you type and the scripts the AI writes are checked for content that breaks our acceptable use rules. When something is blocked we keep a short log entry: the time, which rule it broke, where it was typed, the first part of the blocked text and a scrambled (hashed) form of your network address, so we can improve the filters and spot repeated abuse. Text that passes the checks is not logged. We also count how many videos each network address makes per day to enforce limits. These records are kept for no longer than needed for safety and then deleted. [Confirm the retention period you choose.]
