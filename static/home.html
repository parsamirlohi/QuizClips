Questions people ask

What do I get?

A vertical video (1080 by 1920) with narration, animated text, sound effects and stock footage, ready to upload. It also lists the script and which footage it used.

Where do the questions and footage come from?

Trivia questions come from Open Trivia DB. Scripts for the other video types are written by AI, so check any facts before you post them.

Can I post these videos on my channel?

That's the idea. The footage and questions come from sources that allow it, with credit where required (see the footer). Read the terms for the details that apply to you.

How long does a video take?

Usually a few minutes, depending on the video type and how many questions you choose.

Do I need an account?

Can it post for me?

Not yet. Automatic posting to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels is planned.
